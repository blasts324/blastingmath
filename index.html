      Subway Roller

      Roll down the rails as a little courier robot. Dodge trams, slide under gates, jump the barriers and grab as many sparks as you can. It gets faster the longer you last.

      Your sparks save in your browser and never run out, so spend them on upgrades whenever you want. Magnet, Double Points and Shield all last longer as you level them.

      Controls

      ↑ / spacejump
      ↓slide (or slam down mid-air)
